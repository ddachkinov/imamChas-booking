## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Sign Up**: Visit the registration page and fill in your details
   - Enter your business name
   - Provide your email and create a secure password
   - Accept the terms of service
   - Click "Create Account"

2. **Verify Email**: Check your email inbox for verification link
   - Click the verification link
   - Your account will be activated

3. **First Login**: Log in with your credentials
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click "Sign In"

### Initial Setup

After your first login, you'll be guided through initial setup:

1. **Business Profile**
   - Add your business name, description, and logo
   - Set your business category (salon, spa, fitness, etc.)
   - Configure your timezone

2. **Locations**
   - Add your first business location
   - Enter address and contact information
   - Set business hours for each day

3. **Services**
   - Create services you offer
   - Set pricing and duration
   - Add descriptions and images

4. **Staff**
   - Invite team members
   - Assign roles and permissions
   - Set staff availability

## For Business Owners

### Managing Your Business Profile

1. Navigate to **Admin Dashboard** → **Business Profile**
2. Edit your business information:
   - Business name and description
   - Logo and cover image
   - Contact information
   - Business category
   - Website and social media links
3. Click **Save Changes**

### Adding Locations

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → **Locations**
2. Click **Add Location**
3. Fill in location details:
   - Location name (e.g., "Downtown Branch")
   - Full address
   - Phone number and email
   - Business hours for each day
4. Click **Create Location**

**Business Hours Setup:**
- Toggle each day on/off
- Set opening and closing times
- Add break times if needed
- Copy hours to other days for consistency

### Creating Services

1. Navigate to **Admin Dashboard** → **Services**
2. Click **Add Service**
3. Enter service details:
   - Service name (e.g., "Haircut", "60-Min Massage")
   - Description and benefits
   - Duration (minutes)
   - Price
   - Category
   - Upload service image (optional)
4. Advanced options:
   - Buffer time before/after
   - Maximum bookings per day
   - Deposit required
   - Cancellation policy
5. Click **Create Service**

### Managing Staff

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → **Staff**
2. Click **Add Staff Member**
3. Enter staff information:
   - Full name and email
   - Phone number
   - Role (Staff or Manager)
   - Hire date
   - Bio and photo (optional)
4. Assign:
   - Locations (which locations they work at)
   - Services (which services they can provide)
   - Permissions
5. Click **Create Staff**

**Setting Staff Availability:**
1. Click on staff member
2. Go to **Availability** tab
3. Set weekly schedule:
   - Working days and hours
   - Break times
   - Time off
4. Save changes

### Managing Clients

1. Navigate to **Admin Dashboard** → **Clients**
2. **View Clients**: See all registered clients with:
   - Name and contact information
   - Total appointments
   - Lifetime value
   - Last visit date
3. **Client Details**: Click on client to see:
   - Full profile
   - Appointment history
   - Performance metrics (cancellation rate, no-show rate)
   - Notes and preferences
4. **Add Notes**: Document client preferences, allergies, or special instructions

## For Clients

1. **Access Booking Page**:
   - Visit your business's booking link
   - Or scan QR code at location

2. **Select Service**:
   - Browse available services
   - Filter by category if needed

3. **Choose Staff** (optional):
   - Select specific staff member
   - Or choose "First Available"

4. **Pick Date & Time**:

   - Times shown in Morning, Afternoon, Evening groups

5. **Enter Your Details**:
   - Returning customers: Enter email to auto-fill
   - New customers: Fill in name, email, phone
   - Add special requests (optional)

6. **Confirm Booking**:
   - Review booking summary
   - Accept cancellation policy
   - Click **Confirm Booking**
   - Receive confirmation email

### Managing Your Bookings

2. **Reschedule**:
   - Click "Reschedule" in email

3. **Cancel**:
   - Click "Cancel" in email

## Managing Appointments

### Creating Appointments (Admin/Staff)

1. Go to **Calendar**
2. Click **New Appointment** or press `N`
3. Fill in appointment details:
   - Select client (search by name/email/phone)
   - Choose service
   - Pick staff member
   - Select date and time
   - Add notes
4. Click **Create Appointment**
5. System checks for conflicts
6. Confirmation sent to client automatically

### Viewing Appointment Details

Click on any appointment to view:
- **Client Information**: Name, phone, email
- **Service Details**: Service name, duration, price
- **Staff Assignment**: Who will provide service
- **Status**: Current appointment status
- **Notes**: Any special instructions
- **History**: Status changes and updates

### Changing Appointment Status

**Status Workflow:**
```
Pending → Confirmed → Checked In → In Progress → Completed
           ↓              ↓
        Cancelled    No-Show
```

**Status Definitions:**
- **Pending**: Awaiting confirmation
- **Confirmed**: Appointment confirmed
- **Checked In**: Client arrived
- **In Progress**: Service started
- **Completed**: Service finished
- **Cancelled**: Appointment cancelled
- **No-Show**: Client didn't arrive

### Cancelling Appointments

1. Click on appointment
2. Click **Cancel Appointment**
3. Select cancellation reason:
   - Client requested
   - Staff unavailable
   - Location closed
   - Weather/emergency
   - Other
4. Add notes (optional)
5. Confirm cancellation
6. Client receives cancellation email

Configure how you receive notifications:

**Event Types** (enable per channel):
- Appointment created
- Appointment confirmed

Connect with third-party tools:

**Available Integrations:**

1. **Google Calendar**
   - Sync appointments to Google Calendar

2. **Stripe**
   - Accept online payments
   - Process refunds
   - Manage recurring billing
   - **Setup**: Click "Connect" → Sign in to Stripe → Authorize

3. **Mailgun**
   - Send transactional emails
   - Email templates
   - Delivery tracking
   - **Setup**: Add API key → Configure

4. **Twilio**
   - Send SMS reminders
   - Two-way messaging
   - Delivery reports
   - **Setup**: Add credentials → Test connection

5. **Zapier**
   - Connect 3,000+ apps
   - Automate workflows
   - Custom triggers
   - **Setup**: Click "Connect" → Configure zaps

6. **Outlook Calendar**
   - Sync with Outlook
   - Meeting invites
   - Calendar sharing
   - **Setup**: Click "Connect" → Sign in to Microsoft → Authorize

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In

**Solutions:**
1. Check email spelling
2. Reset password (click "Forgot Password")
3. Check email for verification link
4. Clear browser cache
5. Try different browser
6. Contact support if issue persists

### Not Receiving Emails

**Solutions:**
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Add to safe senders list
3. Verify email address in profile
4. Check notification settings
5. Try different email address
6. Contact support

### Appointment Not Showing

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh browser
2. Check correct date/view
3. Check filters (clear all filters)
4. Verify appointment was confirmed
5. Check spam for confirmation
6. Contact business

### Calendar Sync Issues

**Solutions:**
1. Disconnect and reconnect integration
2. Check calendar permissions
3. Verify timezone settings
4. Re-authorize integration
5. Check internet connection
6. Contact support

### Payment Failed

**Solutions:**
1. Verify card details
2. Check card expiration
3. Ensure sufficient funds
4. Try different card
5. Contact bank
6. Use alternative payment method

### Mobile App Issues

**Solutions:**
1. Update app to latest version
2. Clear app cache
3. Restart phone
4. Reinstall app
5. Check internet connection
6. Contact support

## FAQ

### General

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: We offer three plans: Starter ($29/mo), Professional ($79/mo), and Enterprise ($199/mo). See Billing Settings for details.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
A: Yes, cancel anytime. Access remains until end of billing period.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use bank-level encryption and security measures.

**Q: Do you offer a free trial?**
A: Yes, 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

### Bookings

**Q: How far in advance can clients book?**
A: Default is 90 days. Adjust in Service settings.

**Q: Can I block certain times?**
A: Yes, use "Blocked Time" feature in Calendar.

**Q: Can clients book multiple services?**
A: Yes, they can book multiple appointments.

**Q: What if staff is double-booked?**
A: System prevents double-booking automatically.

### Payments

**Q: When do I receive payment?**
A: Depends on payment processor. Typically 2-7 business days.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Credit cards, debit cards via Stripe integration.

**Q: Can I offer deposits?**
A: Yes, set deposit amount in Service settings.

**Q: How do refunds work?**
A: Process refunds through Billing → Invoices.

### Technical

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions).

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Mobile-optimized web app. Native apps coming soon.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, export appointments (iCal/CSV) and reports.

**Q: What if I need help?**
A: Contact support via email, chat, or phone.

### Notifications

**Q: How do reminders work?**
A: Automatic reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before appointment.

**Q: Can clients opt out of reminders?**
A: Yes, via link in reminder message.

**Q: What notifications are sent?**
A: Booking confirmation, reminders, cancellations, updates.

**Q: Can I customize notification templates?**
A: Yes, customize in Settings → Notifications.
